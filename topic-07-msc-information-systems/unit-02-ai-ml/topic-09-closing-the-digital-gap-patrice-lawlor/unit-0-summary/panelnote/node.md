## Closing the Digital Gap - Patrice Lawlor


### Abstract

This research explores digital literacy in socio-economically disadvantaged areas of Waterford City and how it affects entrepreneurial opportunity. It focuses on access, skills, and confidence as key factors influencing people’s ability to engage in business activity. Using surveys and follow-up interviews, the study examines whether training leads to real confidence and practical use. The research highlights a local gap and aims to support more inclusive, targeted approaches to digital and enterprise development
