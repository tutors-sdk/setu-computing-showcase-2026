### Abstract

This project creates a full brand identity for a niche retro-modern startup hardware company. The primary objective
of this project is to adhere to current niche market needs and fully implement this design through digital and
physical touchpoints. The project details the creation of designs across physical and digital mediums, culmination of
multimedia skills, and how they can be utilised to solve business needs. Leveraging current and more dated
techniques, the project showcases that a single creative problem solver can elevate the design and appeal of
products.
